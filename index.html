Data auto-saves to this browser. Export to back it up or move to another device. Import to restore.
1
Data saves automatically
Every change saves to your browser instantly. Green dot = saved. Works on this device/browser permanently.
GREEN DOT = SAVED
2
New month — resetting goals
Click NEW MONTH next to the goals heading. It asks for the new month name then resets all goal % back to 0 so you start fresh. Your goal titles stay the same.
NEW MONTH BUTTON
3
Signing a client
Change the lead status to Signed in the CRM. The CLIENTS SIGNED counter in the scoreboard auto-updates, and the "Close growth op client" goal progress auto-updates too. Then go to the CLIENTS tab to log their monthly fee.
CRM → STATUS → SIGNED
4
Tracking clients and income
Go to the CLIENTS tab → + ADD CLIENT. Enter name, niche, monthly fee, start date. The MRR (monthly recurring revenue) and total earned update automatically.
CLIENTS TAB
5
Setting & Closing tracker
Go to SETTING & CLOSING tab → LOG A DEAL. Choose Setting or Closing, enter the deal name and amount. The pie chart shows your earning split and totals update live.
SETTING & CLOSING TAB
6
Editing tasks
Click checkbox to tick. Double-click task text to edit it. Click ✕ to delete. Works on both Today and Recurring tasks.
7
Backup
BACKUP tab → EXPORT DATA → copy text → save in Notes or Google Doc. To restore, paste it back → IMPORT.
